Import notification: upload your documents

Upload your supporting documents and we will attempt to extract the information needed for your import notification automatically.

Why an import notification is required

Under the UK's Illegal, Unreported and Unregulated (IUU) fishing regulations, all fish and seafood products imported into Great Britain from non-EU countries must be accompanied by verified documentation. You must submit an import notification before your consignment arrives at the border control post.

Failure to submit a valid notification may result in your consignment being held or refused entry at the border.

Documents you will need to upload

You must provide all of the following documents before you can continue:

Catch Certificate
Confirms the fish were caught legally. Must be issued by the flag state authority of the vessel that caught the fish.
Processing Statement
Required where fish products have been processed or transformed after catch. Issued by the processing country's competent authority.
Non-Manipulation Declaration (NMD)
Confirms that the fish products have not been altered or re-packed since the Processing Statement was issued. Required in most circumstances.

What we will extract automatically

Once you have uploaded your documents, we will attempt to extract the following information to reduce manual data entry:

  • species name and commodity code
  • net weight and product description
  • catch area, catch date and flag state
  • vessel details
  • processing facility name and country
  • Processing Statement and Catch Certificate reference numbers
  • exporter name and export approval number

Not all information may be extracted successfully. Where information cannot be extracted, you will be asked to enter it manually.

Your responsibility

The information extracted from your documents is provided as a suggestion only. You remain responsible for reviewing all extracted information and confirming that it is accurate before submission.

Do not submit information you have not verified. Submitting inaccurate information is a criminal offence under the IUU fishing regulations.

How this reduces manual data entry

By uploading your documents upfront, the service can pre-fill many of the form fields automatically. You will only need to:

  • review the extracted information for accuracy
  • correct any errors or gaps
  • confirm the information is correct before submission

This is expected to significantly reduce the time needed to complete an import notification.
