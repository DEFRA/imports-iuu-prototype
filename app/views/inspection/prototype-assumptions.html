Prototype assumptions

These are research questions for this Alpha prototype, not confirmed requirements.

  1. Who decides that an inspection is required?
  2. Which organisation owns the inspection task?
  3. Can documentary, identity and physical checks be performed by different officers or authorities?
  4. Does an officer need to see original documents beside extracted data?
  5. What evidence must be recorded for each type of check?
  6. Can an officer release a consignment directly?
  7. Who can place or remove a hold?
  8. Who has authority to refuse a consignment?
  9. When must MMO or another competent authority be consulted?
  10. What outcomes and information must be communicated to TRACES NT?
  11. What is the MMO's role at the point of inspection, and when must they be consulted?
  12. What information do port systems need?
  13. How are corrections and disagreements between source systems handled?
  14. Is a digital inspection record sufficient, or is a printable record required?
  15. What audit information must be retained?
