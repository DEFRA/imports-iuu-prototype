Import fish and fish products into Great Britain

Use this service to notify the UK authorities of fish and seafood products you intend to import into Great Britain.

You must submit an import notification before your consignment arrives at the border control post. You will need to provide:

  • your EORI (Economic Operators Registration and Identification) number
  • details of the consignment, including species, product form and weight
  • port of entry and estimated arrival date and time
  • transport details (vessel, flight or road vehicle)
  • supporting documentation, including Catch Certificates

This service covers imports from non-EU countries. Fish imported from EU member states may have different requirements.

Before you start

You must have:

  • a valid EORI number registered with HMRC
  • valid Catch Certificates for all fish and seafood products
  • a Processing Statement if the goods have been processed
  • a Non-Manipulation Declaration if required
