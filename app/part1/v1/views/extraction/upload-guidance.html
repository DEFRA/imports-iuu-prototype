Submit your seafood import notification

Use this service to submit a seafood import notification for fish and fishery products entering Great Britain.

Upload your supporting documents and we'll use them to complete your notification. You'll be able to check and amend the information before submitting.

Before you start

You must submit your seafood import notification before your consignment arrives in Great Britain.

If you do not submit the required information in time, your goods may be delayed, held for inspection or refused entry.

Documents you need

The documents you need depend on the journey your seafood has taken through the supply chain.

In most cases, you'll need to provide:

  • a catch certificate
  • your processing statement
  • a Non-Manipulation Declaration
  • any other additional supporting documents

Catch Certificate

Your Catch Certificate shows that the fish were caught legally in line with fisheries regulations.

It's issued by the authority responsible for the vessel that caught the fish.

Processing Statement

A Processing Statement is needed when fish have been caught then processed. For example, fish that's been filleted, packed, canned or transformed into another product.

Your Processing Statement provides the processing, product and traceability information needed for your notification.

It links the processed product back to the original catch documentation.

Non-Manipulation Declaration

A Non-Manipulation Declaration provides transit and traceability information needed to complete your notification.

You'll need this document if the fish has passed through one or more countries after processing and before import into Great Britain.

Additional documents

The Port Health Authority may ask for additional documentation to support their checks.

Additional supporting documents are not analysed automatically but may be used as part of the review of your notification.

You should have any relevant import documentation available before you start your notification.

What happens when you upload your documents

The service will identify and extract information from your documents to help complete your notification.

This may include:

  • species and product details
  • commodity codes
  • weights and quantities
  • vessel information
  • catch locations and dates
  • country of processing
  • certificate reference numbers
  • importer and exporter details

Check the information carefully

The information extracted from your documents is used to help you complete your notification. You are responsible for its accuracy.

You must check that all information is complete and correct before submitting your notification. If information is missing or incorrect, you can update it before submission.

Providing inaccurate information may result in delays to your consignment and could lead to enforcement action.
